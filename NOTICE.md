# Third-party data notice

SecAwardLens links to and records limited bibliographic facts from IEEE, USENIX,
ACM SIGSAC, the Internet Society/NDSS, Crossref, OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, and
Google Scholar observations retrieved through third-party services such as SerpApi
or ScraperAPI. Those organizations and services do not endorse this project. Their
names, webpages, metadata, and API responses remain subject to their respective
rights and terms.

The application displays the citation provider and retrieval date beside derived
metrics. Source URLs and response digests are retained for auditability.

When configured by the deployer, the public site uses GoatCounter for aggregate
traffic statistics. GoatCounter is an optional analytics service and is not required
to build or use the dataset.
